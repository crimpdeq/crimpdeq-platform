# Palm rest and hand positions

## Fit the palm rest

![Sliding the palm rest onto the rail](../images/rest_on.png)

1. Hold the palm rest with the bolster toward the grip, and line its groove
   up with the rail at the rear end of the base.
2. Slide it along the rail to the position you want.
3. Drop one index key through the slot in each side wing, into the deck slot
   below, head toward the rear. The heads sit flat on the wings.

The rail holds the rest down; the keys stop it from sliding. Always fit both
keys.

## Hand positions

The nine deck slots set the opening from the outside of the finger lip to
the palm face, from 25 mm to 105 mm in 10 mm steps.

![Smallest opening, 25 mm](../images/position_min.png)

![Largest opening, 105 mm](../images/position_max.png)

To change position, unload the device, lift both keys out, slide the rest and
drop the keys into the new slots.

Approximate starting points are 25–45 mm for full crimp or smaller hands,
55–75 mm for half crimp, and 85–105 mm for extended fingers or larger hands.

## Check

- [ ] At every position, both keys drop in and lift out by hand.
- [ ] The rest doesn't rock or lift on the rail.
