# Case, grip and load-cell joints

Push the wrist rest to the palm-post end before starting. That leaves room to
lower the case and grip into the frame.

> [!NOTE]
> The frame has no floor. The case and grip only stay in place once the bolts
> are in, so hold them by hand or rest the case on a block about 24 mm high on
> the table.

## Eye collars

Close the case with its own M2.5 screws; these screws carry no load. Then
push one **aluminium** collar half into each face of both load-cell eyes,
through the Ø18 mm access holes in the case, until its flange seats on the
load cell.

## Case into the frame

![Lower the case into the frame, then slide it left into the fixed clevis](../images/case_in.png)

1. Turn the case so the USB port and switch face the window in the side rail.
2. Lower the case straight down into the frame, to the right of the fixed
   clevis (the pair of plates with a hole at the left end).
3. Slide the case left until its left eye sits between the clevis plates and
   lines up with their holes.

The case must not rub on the frame. There's about 1 mm clearance to each
clevis plate.

## Finger grip

![Lower the grip in, pocket up, then slide it left over the right end of the case](../images/grip_in.png)

1. Hold the grip with the finger pocket facing up.
2. Lower it into the frame just to the right of the case until its top is
   level with the frame top. Its two plates (cheeks) then sit at the height
   of the case's top and bottom.
3. Slide it left so the cheeks go over and under the right end of the case,
   until the cheek holes line up with the right eye. Hold it there until its
   sleeve is in.

## Sleeves, bolts, washers and nuts

Do this for both joints, the fixed clevis on the left and the grip on the
right. Keep the frame standing on its feet, so the bolt heads end up on top
and the nuts underneath.

![Exploded joint: bolt and washer from the top, steel sleeve through the joint, washer and nut from below](../images/bolt_stack.png)

1. Push a **steel** sleeve down through the upper cheek, the collars in the
   load-cell eye and the lower cheek. It should end flush with both cheek
   faces.
2. Put a washer under the bolt head and push the bolt down through the sleeve
   from the top.
3. Tip the frame onto its side or reach underneath. Put the second washer on
   the bolt tip and screw on the nyloc nut.
4. Tighten until the washers seat on the cheek faces.

![Finished joint in section](../images/bolt_done.png)

> [!WARNING]
> The bolts only retain the joints; the sleeves carry the load. Don't tighten
> them so hard that the cheeks bow into the case.

## Check the joints

- [ ] The bolt thread passes fully through each nyloc nut.
- [ ] The grip can move a little along the frame without the case touching the
      grip or the frame walls.
- [ ] The USB port and switch are reachable through the side-rail window.
- [ ] On a flat table only the four feet touch. The nuts stay clear.
