You already have the Crimpdeq v2 (case, board and 50 kg load cell) and its
M2.5 case screws. The case screws carry no load.

The M8 bolts retain the load-cell joints. The M5 screws clamp the frame's two
lap joints. The wrist pins positively lock the pad against horizontal
movement; they do not rely on surface friction.

## Load-transfer inserts

**Do not use printed sleeves or collars.** The long sleeves carry bending
across the enclosure, and both sleeve bending and collar bearing exceed the
printed-material screening limits (see
[Structural screening](../design/structural.md)).

| Insert | Nominal dimensions (mm) |
| --- | --- |
| Steel sleeve, quantity 2 | Ø12.4 outside, Ø8.4 bore, 45 long |
| Aluminium collar half, quantity 4 | Ø16.4 body, Ø12.7 bore, 1.9 body length |
| Collar flange | Ø17.6, 0.5 thick; 2.4 total half length |

These are nominal dimensions, not a toleranced production drawing. Have the
machinist verify sliding fits, deburr edges, and check the real sensor and
clevises before manufacture.

## Tools and consumables

- 4 mm hex key for the M5 screws, and 13 mm spanners for the M8 nuts
- Sandpaper, 220–400 grit, for support scars and tight holes
- Filament for both plates (see [Printing](printing.md))
