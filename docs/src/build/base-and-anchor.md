# Base and anchor

## Join the base halves

![Lowering the rear half onto the front half](../images/join_base.png)

1. Stand the front half on a flat table, deck up.
2. Hold the rear half above it, rail up, with its two joint sockets over the
   front half's two dovetail tongues.
3. Lower it straight down until both halves stand on the table.

The joint faces meet across the full width and the decks are flush. Under
load the two halves press against each other, so the joint needs no glue or
screws.

## Fit the anchor block

![Dropping the anchor block into its pocket](../images/anchor_in.png)

Drop the anchor block into the pocket at the outer end of the front half:
its tall end wall toward the end of the base, the lug toward the middle.
It sits on the pocket floor, and its seat stands just above the deck, where
the left end of the load cell will rest.
