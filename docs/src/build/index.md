# Build

![Assembled platform](../images/overview.png)

This part of the book explains how to print and assemble the Crimpdeq hand
dynamometer on a Bambu Lab A1, or any printer with a 256 mm bed, using steel
hardware and machined load-cell inserts.

> [!CAUTION]
> **The design has not been load-tested.** Physical validation of the fit,
> the printed parts and the bolted frame joint is still pending. Do not pull
> on the device outside a separately approved, guarded validation setup, and
> never load the sensor above its 50 kg rating.

## What you are building

The dynamometer holds the Crimpdeq v2 case, with its 50 kg load cell, between
two supports:

- a **finger grip** (blue) with a hangboard-style pocket, bolted to the right
  load-cell eye
- a **wrist rest** (orange) with a palm bolster and heel deck, which slides
  along the frame rails and locks into one of nine positions

The **frame** (grey) holds the left load-cell eye in a fixed clevis. It is
printed as two halves, dark and light grey in the pictures, joined by a
bolted half-lap in each rail. The USB port and switch stay reachable through a
window in the side rail.

## Overall workflow

1. [Buy](what-to-buy.md) the hardware and machined inserts.
2. [Print](printing.md) Plate 2 (wrist rest and grip), then Plate 1 (frame
   halves), after the [slicer checks](slicer-checks.md).
3. [Check the parts](check-the-parts.md) against the hardware.
4. Slide the wrist rest onto the right frame half, then
   [bolt the halves together](frame-and-wrist-rest.md).
5. Fit the [case, grip and load-cell joints](case-grip-and-bolts.md).
6. Fit the [wrist pins](wrist-pins-and-positions.md) and try every hand
   position.
7. Run the [final checks](checks-and-limits.md) and record your feedback.
