# Fit trial, feedback and limits

> [!CAUTION]
> Rest your hand in place only. **Do not pull, squeeze hard, hang from it or
> lean on it.**

## Fit checks

- [ ] **All nine positions:** the pins go in easily each time, and the heel
      deck passes cleanly through the channel in the palm post.
- [ ] **Pin heads:** you can grip and pull each pin at every position. The gap
      to the bolster ends is only about 1.5 mm.
- [ ] **Case access:** the USB port and switch are reachable at every position.
- [ ] **Tabletop:** only the four feet and the two seam-brace legs touch. The
      nuts, bolt tips, pin tips and paperclips stay clear.
- [ ] **Case clearance:** the case doesn't touch the frame, grip or wrist rest.
- [ ] **Glue joint:** no visible gap or step at the rail joints.
- [ ] **Grip level:** the grip's top is level with the frame top on both sides
      and it rests on the brace pads, not on the table.

## Comfort checks

Put your fingers in the pocket and the heel of your palm on the wrist rest,
without pulling.

- [ ] The finger pocket depth and lip feel natural.
- [ ] The palm bolster height suits your hand: not too high, not too low.
- [ ] The bolster and deck edges are smooth enough; nothing digs in.
- [ ] The heel deck supports your wrist without pressure points.
- [ ] Note which openings suit full crimp, half crimp and open hand.

## Taking it apart

1. Remove the paperclips and pull out both wrist pins.
2. Unscrew both nuts counter-clockwise and push out the bolts and spacers.
3. Slide the grip right and lift it out, then do the same with the case.
4. The wrist rest stays on the glued frame.

## Known limits

> [!WARNING]
> - **Fit only.** The glued frame, printed hardware and printed inserts have no
>   load rating. The design has not been load-tested.
> - **The one-piece frame can't be assembled yet.** The wrist rest's arms wrap
>   both rails, and the one-piece frame has closed posts at both ends, so the
>   rest can't get onto the rails. The split frame works only because the rest
>   goes on before gluing. A loaded version needs a design change first, such as
>   a bolt-on palm post or detachable lower arms.
> - **Physical validation is still pending:** fit with the real case, comfort,
>   and any load testing.

A loaded version will need all of these:

- a one-piece frame printed on a bed larger than 256 mm, or by a print service
- the design fix above
- steel M8 hardware and rated ball-lock pins
- machined steel sleeves and aluminium collars
- a separate, guarded load-validation plan that never exceeds the 50 kg
  load-cell rating
