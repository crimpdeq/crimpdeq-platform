# Assembly

> [!WARNING]
> Steps 6 and 7 are blocked on the one-piece frame until the design change in
> [Loaded version](index.md) is made.

1. Install one **metal** flanged collar half from each face of both load-cell
   eyes.
2. Place the closed Crimpdeq enclosure in the fixed frame.
3. Position the moving hangboard pocket over the right eye with its opening
   facing `+Z`.
4. Slide one **steel** pin sleeve through each clevis, case opening, and
   collar pair.
5. Install bolts, washers, and nyloc nuts.
6. Place the saddle's upright palm face between the hangboard pocket and the
   fixed outer post, with its heel deck above the frame extending away from
   the fingers. The mounting arms straddle the rails above and below.
7. Select the opening, align both pad holes with the corresponding frame index
   holes, and push one ball-lock pin through each arm, rail, and arm stack.
8. Confirm that both pins' locking balls are fully extended below the lower
   arms, tether each pin to the adjacent anchor hole, and tug-test both pins.
9. To readjust, fully unload the device, press each pin's release button, pull
   the pins by hand, reposition the pad, and reinsert and tug-test both pins.
10. Verify switch and USB access and that the enclosure is clear of
    load-bearing parts. Tare the instrument unloaded.

Loading requires a separately approved, guarded validation setup. Stop for any
unexpected motion or shell contact.
