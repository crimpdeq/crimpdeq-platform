# Loaded version

> [!CAUTION]
> **The loaded version cannot be built yet.** The wrist rest's arms wrap both
> rails, and the one-piece frame has closed posts at both ends, so the rest
> cannot be fitted onto its rails. The split fit-prototype frame works only
> because the rest goes on before gluing. A loaded build first needs a design
> change, such as a bolt-on palm post or detachable lower arms (see
> [Known limits](../fit-prototype/fit-trial-and-feedback.md#known-limits)).

These pages record the hardware, printing, and assembly requirements for a
future load-bearing build, so that the fit prototype is checked against the
right parts. A loaded build needs a one-piece frame printed on a bed larger
than 256 mm or by a print service, steel M8 hardware and rated ball-lock pins,
machined steel sleeves and aluminium collars, and a separate, guarded
load-validation plan that never exceeds the 50 kg load-cell rating.

Retire printed load-bearing parts after any overload, drop, cracking,
whitening, or permanent deformation. Never use printed quick-release pins, and
never apply load unless both steel pins are fully locked.
