# Printing

1. Print the unloaded sleeve replicas (`pin_spacer_pair`) first, upright at
   100% infill. Label them as fit-only and remove them before installing the
   metal inserts. There are no printed collar replicas.
2. Check their fit against the actual sensor and frame, then obtain the metal
   inserts. Inspect assembled joint play; nominal CAD clearance is not a fit
   qualification.
3. The `frame` export places its flat upper rail faces on the bed, with the
   four feet pointing upward. Support the bridge over the relieved right-post
   channel from the bed, and remove support material without gouging its
   clearance floor.
4. Print the `grip` on a broad Z face at 100% infill, with supports below its
   upper clevis cheek and finger-pocket rim.
5. The `wrist_rest` export stands on its two flat mounting arms, with the palm
   cradle facing upward. Support the overhanging heel deck from below, keep
   support scars off the palm and heel surfaces and the bolster, and smooth
   any brim or seam at the contact edges. Inspect layer adhesion where the
   upper arms enter the bolster before fitting the pins.
6. Use at least 6 perimeters and 6 top/bottom layers, 40–60% infill for the
   frame, and 100% for the grip and wrist rest.

PETG or ASA may be used for unloaded fit prototypes. PA-CF or PET-CF may be
candidates for structural evaluation, but a filament name or infill setting
does not establish strength. Validate coupons in the intended print
orientation.
