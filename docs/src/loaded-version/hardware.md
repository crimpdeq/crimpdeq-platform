## Load-transfer inserts

**Do not use printed spacers or collars under load.** The long sleeves carry
bending across the enclosure, and both sleeve bending and collar bearing
exceed the printed-material screening limits (see
[Structural screening](../design/structural.md)).

| Insert | Nominal dimensions (mm) |
| --- | --- |
| Steel sleeve, quantity 2 | Ø12.4 outside, Ø8.4 bore, 45 long |
| Aluminium collar half, quantity 4 | Ø16.4 body, Ø12.7 bore, 1.9 body length |
| Collar flange | Ø17.6, 0.5 thick; 2.4 total half length |

These are nominal fit-model dimensions, not a toleranced production drawing.
Have the machinist verify sliding fits, deburr edges, and check the real
sensor and clevises before manufacture. Printed insert exports are **unloaded
fit replicas only**; do not replace metal inserts with higher-infill printed
ones. Do not tighten bolts in a way that bows the cheeks into the enclosure.
